READINESS CAVEAT -- READ THIS BEFORE TRUSTING A GREEN DEPLOYMENT
----------------------------------------------
/readyz proves only that Postgres is reachable and this binary's embedded
migrations match what's applied. It deliberately excludes the embedder,
the forge, the policy socket, the ingest pool, and the sync scheduler.

A green readiness probe is NOT proof that repo sync or code ingest are
alive. A dead sync scheduler or ingest pool can log exactly one error line
and otherwise look, from the outside, identical to "quiet because there's
nothing to do." To actually check:

  - Sync scheduler: RepoAdminService.ListRepos/GetRepo -- SyncStatus.state
    and last_synced_at should be advancing roughly every
    {{ .Values.config.syncInterval }} across every repo.
  - Ingest pool: RepoAdminService.ListIngestJobs?status=queued -- a queued
    count that only grows, with nothing transitioning to running, means
    the pool is dead, not merely busy.

See docs/deployment-spec.md's "The Readiness Caveat" section for the full
detail (loam-lae, loam-ymyq).
